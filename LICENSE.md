## 9. Privacy: what leaves your computer

We built Syphony so that your content stays on computers you choose. This is what does leave yours, and who sees it. For the counts below, we are the controller under the EU's data protection law (GDPR).

- **To the people you share with:** the pages, files, meetings and live edits you share, sealed on your computer so only those people can open them.
- **Sealed copies:** while a friend you share with is offline, your changes can wait for them, sealed, on up to four other computers: your own, your friends', or those of people who chose to keep copies for others. Your computer may keep sealed changes for others in the same way, for up to two weeks. Nobody who keeps them can read them.
- **Finding each other:** to reach a friend, your computer asks public introducers where their computer is, and those introducers see your computer's network (IP) address, as on any internet connection. When computers can't reach each other directly, sealed traffic may go through the Tor network or through another Syphony user's computer.
- **Updates:** Syphony fetches new versions from GitHub (Microsoft), which sees your network address.
- **Counts:** unless you switch off *Count this computer* in Settings › Diagnostics, Syphony sends, once a day it is used, a random number made on your computer, its version and platform, the days it was used and how often each part of Syphony was used. Never your content, names or anything you typed. Our counter on Cloudflare receives them; it uses your network address only to limit how often it is asked, and keeps it nowhere. We count to learn which parts of Syphony people use, which is our legitimate interest (GDPR article 6(1)(f)), and every number we make from the counts is public at [syphony.net/stats](https://syphony.net/stats). Each computer's daily rows are deleted after 400 days. Switching counting off deletes the random number and the counts on your computer, and nothing more is sent.
- **Services you connect:** an AI service or another program you connect gets what you send it, under its own terms.
- **This website** is hosted by Google (Firebase) and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which see your network address. It sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

You have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to the counting, which you can also do yourself by switching it off. We can't tell your counts apart from anybody else's without the random number on your computer, so usually we hold nothing we can find as yours. If you think we handle data wrongly, tell us (section 21), or complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium, the [Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be).
